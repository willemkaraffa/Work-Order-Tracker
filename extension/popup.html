How to capture from AMH:

1. Open any work order on www.amh.com
2. Click the green 📋 Capture WO button (top-right of page)
3. WO is saved to the Saved tab automatically
4. Click Send All to Tracker when ready

Sync: Click ↻ Sync to pull your current statuses, priorities, and types from the tracker app into the extension.

Mappings: Define how AMH portal status text maps to your tracker statuses.
